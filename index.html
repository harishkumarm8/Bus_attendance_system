Daily Attendance

Student Management

Add, edit, and manage student bus assignments. Filter by bus, department, year, or drop point.

Attendance Reports

Generate and export detailed PDF reports by date, bus, or student. Download with one click.

ERP Sync

Synchronize students directly from the college ERP system. Keeps student data always up to date.

Faculty Access Control

Grant or revoke bus-specific access to faculty staff. Each staff member sees only their assigned bus.

Drop Point Tracking

Manage multiple drop points per bus. Track student drop point status (Active / Inactive).
